# Microsoft Integrates Teradata's Enterprise AI with OneLake

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Category:** Data Engineering

## What Happened

On September 2, 2026, Teradata announced a deep integration between its Autonomous Knowledge Platform and Microsoft's OneLake, enabling enterprise customers to run Teradata's high-performance enterprise AI directly on data stored in OneLake without the need for extract, transform, and load (ETL) pipelines, data duplication, or migration. ([nasdaq.com](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/teradata-brings-enterprise-ai-directly-microsoft-onelake-2026-09-02?utm_source=openai))

## Why It Matters

This integration allows organizations to perform advanced AI analytics on their existing data infrastructure, reducing the complexity and overhead associated with data movement and transformation. By leveraging the combined capabilities of Teradata and Microsoft OneLake, enterprises can achieve more efficient and scalable AI deployments, leading to faster insights and improved decision-making processes.
