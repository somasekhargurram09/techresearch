# Microsoft Integrates Fabric with Copilot to Enhance AI Capabilities

**Date:** 2026-09-29
**Category:** Data Engineering, AI

## What Happened

Microsoft has announced an integration between Fabric IQ and Microsoft Copilot, aiming to transform Fabric into a foundational layer for AI applications. This integration enables Fabric to serve as a context layer for AI agents, providing consistent business logic and facilitating seamless AI-driven processes. Additionally, Microsoft introduced IQ sharing, allowing users to share governed data and context across their organization and with third parties. The company also unveiled new capabilities in Fabric Apps, enabling developers to connect applications directly to Fabric warehouses, SQL databases, lakehouses, and Power BI semantic models without data duplication.
