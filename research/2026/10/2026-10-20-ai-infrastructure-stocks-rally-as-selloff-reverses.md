# AI Infrastructure Stocks Rally as Selloff Reverses

**Date:** 2026-10-19  
**Category:** Data Engineering, AI, US Stocks

## What Happened

On October 19, 2026, AI infrastructure stocks experienced a significant rebound following a recent selloff. Applied Digital's stock surged by 8%, while TeraWulf and IREN each gained 6%. This recovery reversed the previous week's downturn, which had been driven by concerns over debt-financed AI infrastructure investments. ([finance.yahoo.com](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/ai-infrastructure-stocks-rally-selloff-130219230.html?utm_source=openai))

## Why It Matters

The volatility in AI infrastructure stocks underscores the market's sensitivity to the financial structures supporting AI development. The recent selloff was primarily attributed to worries about the sustainability of AI investments heavily financed through debt. The rebound suggests that investors are reassessing the long-term viability of these companies, potentially stabilizing the sector. ([finance.yahoo.com](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/ai-infrastructure-stocks-rally-selloff-130219230.html?utm_source=openai))

## Details

The selloff in AI infrastructure stocks was triggered by concerns over the sustainability of debt-financed AI investments. Companies like CoreWeave saw significant declines, with bond yields approaching 15% and credit default swaps reaching distress levels. This raised questions about the viability of AI infrastructure projects that rely heavily on borrowed funds. ([clarisse.news](https://clarisse.news/issues/ai-chip-stocks-global-selloff-october-2026?utm_source=openai))

The subsequent rally indicates a shift in investor sentiment, with a renewed focus on the potential of AI infrastructure companies. Applied Digital, TeraWulf, and IREN's gains reflect this optimism, suggesting that the market may be stabilizing after the recent volatility.
