# NetApp Unveils Novus Storage Architecture to Address AI Data Demands

**Date:** 2026-10-01  
**Category:** Data Engineering

## What Happened

At the NetApp Insight 2026 conference in Las Vegas, CEO George Kurian introduced NetApp Novus, a new storage architecture designed to meet the high-performance requirements of AI factories. Novus offers throughput up to 100Tbps and zettabyte-scale capability, aiming to eliminate performance bottlenecks in GPU-intensive environments. The system disaggregates metadata and scales storage independently, enhancing efficiency and reducing power consumption by 31%. NetApp collaborated with partners like Lambda and Nvidia to demonstrate real-world use cases for Novus, focusing on optimizing GPU utilization and minimizing energy waste. ## Why It Matters

As AI applications become more complex, the demand for robust data infrastructure grows. Traditional storage solutions often struggle to keep up with the performance needs of AI workloads, leading to inefficiencies and increased operational costs. NetApp's Novus addresses these challenges by providing a scalable and efficient storage solution tailored for AI environments, potentially setting a new industry standard for AI-optimized data engineering.
