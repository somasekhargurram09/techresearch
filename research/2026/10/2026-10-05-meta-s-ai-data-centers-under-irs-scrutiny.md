# Meta's AI Data Centers Under IRS Scrutiny

**Date:** 2026-10-05  
**Category:** Data Engineering, AI, US Stocks

## What Happened

Meta Platforms Inc. has been under scrutiny by the U.S. Internal Revenue Service (IRS) for classifying certain AI data centers as "experimental." This classification enabled Meta to claim nearly $6 billion in tax credits over two years. By designating its AI facilities as "pilot models," Meta qualified for research and development tax incentives intended to support innovation with potential financial risks. The company saved $2 billion in 2024 and $3.9 billion in 2025 using this tax strategy, making it the largest claimant of this specific credit. ([techradar.com](https://www.techradar.com/pro/meta-told-the-irs-its-ai-data-centers-are-experimental-to-shave-usd6-billion-off-its-tax-bill?utm_source=openai))

## Why It Matters

The IRS's investigation into Meta's tax strategy raises questions about the legitimacy of such claims and the broader implications for corporate tax practices. The outcome of this scrutiny could influence how other tech companies approach tax incentives related to AI infrastructure investments. Additionally, the investigation highlights the complexities and potential risks associated with large-scale AI data center operations.
