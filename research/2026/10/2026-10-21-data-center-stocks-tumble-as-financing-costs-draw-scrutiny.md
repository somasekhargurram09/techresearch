# Data Center Stocks Tumble as Financing Costs Draw Scrutiny

**Date:** 2026-10-15  
**Category:** Data Engineering, US Stocks

## What Happened

On October 15, 2026, shares of data center companies Cipher Digital and Applied Digital experienced significant declines, falling 9% and 6% respectively. This downturn was attributed to growing investor concerns over the rising costs associated with financing data center expansions. The market is increasingly scrutinizing the ability of these companies to scale operations without incurring substantial debt or shareholder dilution. ## Why It Matters

The decline in data center stocks underscores a broader market reassessment of the financial sustainability of companies heavily investing in infrastructure to meet the surging demand for AI and cloud computing services. As these companies expand their data centers, the associated financing costs and potential for dilution are becoming critical factors influencing investor sentiment and stock performance. ## Details

Cipher Digital, a company specializing in high-performance computing infrastructure, priced a zero-coupon convertible note offering to fund its Barber Lake HPC expansion and new development sites. This move has intensified concerns about the company's ability to manage debt and deliver on its expansion plans without adversely affecting shareholder value.
